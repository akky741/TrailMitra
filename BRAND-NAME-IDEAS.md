# Brand name shortlist

These are creative name ideas for a Maharashtra-focused adventure and trekking company. They are suggestions, not trademark or domain clearance.

1. Sahyadri Quest — strong regional identity; broad enough for trekking, camping and tours.
2. TrailMitra — friendly, memorable and suitable for a community-led travel brand.
3. WildSahyadri — nature-forward and strongly Maharashtra-focused.
4. TrekVeda — distinctive adventure feel; check similar existing brands carefully.
5. Ghats & Trails — descriptive and premium; can expand beyond trekking.
6. RidgeRoam — short, modern and suitable for domestic/international tours.
7. PahadPath — easy to associate with mountain journeys.
8. RoamSutra — broad travel and experience positioning.
9. Sahyadri Stories — warm, experiential and content-friendly.
10. TerraTrek Adventures — professional and expandable.

Suggested domain patterns to check after selecting a name:
- brandname.in
- brandname.com
- brandname.co.in

Before buying, check exact availability through a domain registrar, trademark conflicts in relevant travel/tourism classes, social handles, and spelling/pronunciation in English, Marathi and Hindi. Domain availability can change at any time.
